Harbour Bend · fictional district

Make a name in Harbour Bend. Talk to the people with jobs (look for the orange !), win Kunle's race, carry Mama Titi's fragile parcel, and drive Emeka's danfo round the bus stops. Keep your car in one piece, or Chidi's Workshop will fix it for a price. Finish Harbour Bend and the Old Ferry Bridge reopens to Marina Point. Earn medals with Coach Kola at the Harbour Bend Range, and keep your heat down: the patrol unit doesn't like gunfire on the street. Over the causeway at Lagoon Point Airfield, Captain Ade runs Flight School, and Ebi at the Marina jetty has a speedboat that needs a fast driver. Press J for the mission list: break a crew member out of the holding centre, race the lagoon shore, or deliver hot pepper soup. Harbour Heights on the square has its doors open.
